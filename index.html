Energy & Environment

Pure air, Powered by Algae.

Dense city streets trap nitrogen dioxide and particulate matter, putting residents, especially people with asthma, at risk. We are building a solar-powered photobioreactor that cleans the air where people actually breathe it.

See how it worksOur Target

Product

A living air purifier

Our photobioreactor uses Chlorella microalgae, kept growing by solar-powered LEDs, to take pollutants out of city air and give back clean oxygen.

1

Air in

An intake vent draws polluted street-level air into the unit.

2

Algae at work

Chlorella uses light and the nitrogen in NO₂, and captures CO₂ through photosynthesis.

3

Clean air out

An exhaust vent releases cleaner air and fresh oxygen back into the street.

4

Harvest & reuse

Harvested biomass becomes a nutrient-rich fertiliser for local farms.

Self-powered

Solar-powered internal LEDs keep the culture growing continuously.

Non-toxic

A natural process with no harsh chemicals in the air we release.

Built for streets

Weather-resistant polycarbonate shell and automated monitoring.
