Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

About this notice

This notice explains how personal information is handled when you visit the Chlorology project website or contact the project team.

For privacy questions, contact chlorology@oxf.gdst.net .

Information you provide

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.

Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for details of GitHub’s processing.

Fonts and the 3D viewer

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and software for its interactive 3D viewer from Google-hosted servers. Your browser sends technical information, such as your IP address and request headers, when requesting these files. These providers may process information outside the UK.

See Google’s Privacy Policy for more information.

Cookies and analytics

The website code does not include advertising trackers or analytics tools, and we do not intentionally set non-essential cookies. Hosting and external services may process technical information as described above.

Why we process information

Where we process personal information to respond to enquiries or administer the website, we rely on our legitimate interests in communicating about the project and maintaining a functioning, secure website.

Retention and sharing

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as needed to respond and manage relevant follow-up. Information handled by hosting, email, and external service providers is subject to their applicable retention policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us to make a request.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office .

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our website or handling of personal information changes.
